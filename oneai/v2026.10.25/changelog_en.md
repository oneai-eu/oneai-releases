# OneAI v2026.10.25 — More Control Over AI and Knowledge

**Release Date:** 2026-10-25

## Highlights

- **Connect your own AI models with BYOK:** Connect your preferred model providers and use your own credentials. This gives you greater control over which AI models are available in OneAI.
