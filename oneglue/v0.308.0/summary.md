OneGlue v0.308.0: All oneAI settings in one place. Under “Integrations → oneAI” you now find the new switch “Allow oneAI integration”, the customer agents and the oneAI server. Individual organizations can get their own oneAI server, and a oneAI key is only ever sent to its own server. The old Tenant pairing no longer exists; you connect oneAI with your personal API key. Note for admins: while the switch is off, oneAI shows a misleading hint “create a new key” until its next update; switching it back on is enough.
