# OneGlue v0.308.0 — What's New

**Released:** 2026-11-15

**All oneAI settings in one place**

- Under “Integrations → oneAI” you now find every setting for the oneAI connection: the new switch “Allow oneAI integration”, the customer agents and the oneAI server. Individual organizations can get their own oneAI server; a oneAI key is only ever sent to its own server. The old connection through the Tenant pairing no longer exists. You connect oneAI with your personal API key.
- *Note for admins:* While the switch is off, oneAI shows a misleading hint “create a new key” until its next update. Switching it back on is enough; you do not need a new key.
