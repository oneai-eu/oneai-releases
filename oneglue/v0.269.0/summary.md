OneGlue v0.269.0 adds a per-user access report (Settings → Users, also works for disabled/former users) showing which passwords a user revealed as a ready rotation list, what they viewed, what they were denied, and their login/session/role history, exportable as JSON or CSV — ideal for offboarding or an incident investigation; the audit log now shows the actor by name, its user filter finds disabled users, and CSV exports include object names; and promoting an outside-domain user to a tenant-wide role now e-mails all tenant admins.
