# OneGlue v0.276.0 — What's New

**Released:** 2026-09-27

**Report a problem or ask for a feature without leaving OneGlue — and delete attachments at last, without the fear of losing something for good.**

## Support requests and feature ideas, straight from the app

- **Open "Support & feature request"** in your profile menu and tell us what is going on in a subject and a message. No detour through e-mail, no hunting for the right address.
- **Tell us which it is:** a support case or a feature request. That choice decides who picks it up and how quickly — an outage is handled differently from a good idea.
- **The technical context of the page you were on is sent along automatically.** You do not need to look up version numbers or describe where exactly you were — which usually saves the first round of questions.
- **Your request gets its own case number** and a confirmation by e-mail. Under "My requests" you can see at any time what you reported and whether it was delivered.
- **When the application runs into an error**, a "Report this problem" button appears right there, with the context of the error already filled in.
- Your message is processed for support purposes; the form names the providers involved before you send. **Never put passwords or credentials into the form** — we do not need them, and they cannot be removed from a message once it has been sent.

## Deleting attachments — with a trash

- **Attachments can now be deleted.** Until now the attempt failed, and uploaded files stayed forever.
- **Deleted files go to the trash** instead of disappearing straight away. There you can see when they were deleted and how long they remain restorable — so a slip of the hand costs one click instead of your afternoon.
- This covers every kind of attachment: on documents, on passwords, on certificates and on contacts.

## Smaller fixes

- Reliability, backup and logging improvements behind the scenes.
