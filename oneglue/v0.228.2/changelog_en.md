# OneGlue v0.228.2 — What's New

**Release Date:** 2026-07-27

**A home for your passkeys.** OneGlue can now store and organize the passkeys your team uses to sign in to third-party websites — personal and shared — with the same vault-grade protection, permissions, and audit trail as your passwords. A companion Chromium browser extension that lets you sign in with a stored passkey is **rolling out now**.

## Your passkeys, organized

- **Store passkeys for the sites you use** — grouped automatically by website, with personal and shared/team passkeys kept clearly apart.
- **Find the right one fast** — mark favorites, see when each was last used, filter by website.
- **Manage them like any other secret** — rename, share with specific colleagues (per-person permissions), revoke, restore from trash.

## Sign in with the browser extension (rolling out)

- **A Chromium extension** lets a stored passkey sign you in on a real website — so a passkey isn't stuck on one device.
- **Anti-phishing by design** — connecting a browser uses a short confirmation code shown inside the extension, so a connection you didn't start can't quietly attach itself.

## Protected like everything else in OneGlue

- **Passkeys are encrypted at rest** under your organization's key and are only ever used inside OneGlue's trust zone — never handed to outside services, logs, or backups.
- **The same permissions and approvals** that guard your passwords guard your shared passkeys, including multi-person approval where configured.
