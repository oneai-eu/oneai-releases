OneGlue v0.228.2 gives your team a home for the passkeys they use to sign in to third-party websites — personal and shared — stored and organized just like passwords, grouped automatically by site with favorites and last-used tracking. Manage them like any other secret: rename, share with specific colleagues via per-person permissions, revoke, or restore from trash. A companion Chromium browser extension that signs you in with a stored passkey on a real website is rolling out now, with anti-phishing built in — connecting a browser requires a short confirmation code shown inside the extension. Passkeys are encrypted at rest under your organization's key, never leave OneGlue's trust zone, and are governed by the same permissions and approvals (including multi-person approval where configured) as everything else in your vault.
