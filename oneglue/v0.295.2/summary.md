OneGlue v0.295.2: You now grant access by organization, to several people at once and, if you like, for a limited time. Roles & groups by organization. Search for an organization by name and see right away who is an admin, contributor or viewer there. Expanded, the row shows the people, since when they have access, who added them and until when the role applies. Grant access in one go. The new "Grant access" assistant gives several people access to several organizations at the same time: choose people, choose organizations, choose a role, optionally add an end date and a reason, review, apply. Before applying you see exactly what is new and what changes. You can open it from "Users & Roles", from "Roles & Groups" and from the members of an organization ("Add user"). Time-limited access. Roles in an organization can have an end date. After that date they no longer apply – automatically, with no follow-up work. A clearer "Users & Roles" page. At a glance: type of account (staff, external, guest), tenant role, access to organizations, status and last sign-in – with filters. A side panel shows all roles of a person. "Change role" explains each role in one sentence. Guests, clearly defined. Guests only see entries that were shared with them individually. If a guest should see whole organizations, a tenant admin converts them into a user without a tenant role. The account's expiry date stays and can be changed at any time. "How access works". A short explanation right in the app shows which role applies to what – ready to pass on to your team. Clear limits for organization admins. They grant access only in their own organization and at most as contributor. Contributors see their buttons. Anyone who is a contributor in an organization now sees the buttons for creating and editing there. Agent, security and operations: a new agent with current security updates; on Windows the taskbar icon now shows the agent's status. Security updates for all components we use. Users without a tenant role now only see the settings and lists of their own organizations.
