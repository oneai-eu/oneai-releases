# OneGlue v0.285.1 — What's New

**Released:** 2026-10-17

**What your customers enter during onboarding is now documented properly, right away.**

- **Real entries instead of free text.** Services such as email/Office, backup, website, business software, internet connection and remote access become their own entries, the web address becomes a domain, contacts get a role and a primary contact, devices get their device type. Login details are attached to the device or service they belong to.
- **"From the customer, unchecked".** Everything the customer entered is marked. On the new review list per organization you accept entries one by one or all at once.
- **The questions you actually need — all optional.** Approval and emergency contact, notes for on-site visits, firewall, switch and UPS, registrar and DNS, Microsoft 365, remote access, previous IT provider (including "still has access") and internet contract details.
- **Login details with a note and a 2FA hint.** Fixed rows for Wi-Fi, registrar, Windows administrator and Microsoft 365 administrator. The note is stored encrypted and shown only together with the password. Network plans also as `.vsdx`, `.drawio` and `.zip`.
- **No more dead ends.** "Resend link" keeps the progress so far, "Offer access again" without a new questionnaire, one reminder three days before the deadline and a clean close afterwards — no data is deleted.
- **You hear about it immediately** when a customer sets up their account. Your customers land directly in their own organization after signing in.
- **Security:** onboarding invitations are now sent by your staff only (customers still invite colleagues through the normal member invitation). Connections to addresses a customer entered work only after your review. Request limits now apply per sender.
