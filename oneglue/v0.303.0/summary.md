OneGlue v0.303.0: External people with organization roles now see and reveal what their role allows – and anyone who loses a Passkey has a way back. Important: Organization viewers, organization contributors and tenant viewers can now reveal all passwords with default access in their organization(s). For entries that only certain people should see, please set them to "restricted" ("Reveal default → Restricted"). External people with organization roles. They now see and reveal what their role allows. Imported entries behave like entries created in OneGlue. A way back after a lost Passkey – for every account in a tenant. You receive an e-mailed link to register a new Passkey. For accounts in a tenant, recovery codes alone no longer create a Passkey. Invitations always match the current access. If an admin changes the role or blocks the account before the invitation is redeemed, the invitation is void. A clear warning before far-reaching roles. Before a tenant-wide role is given, OneGlue shows with numbers what the role does. Addresses outside your own domains need an additional, separate confirmation. Reactivating and re-inviting works again. This includes accounts and memberships with an end date. Expired accounts see a clear notice. Access ends when access ends. Open remote sessions end about one minute at the latest after access is removed. In the browser, the change applies from the next action. Tenant roles are assigned in OneGlue, not through Microsoft Entra groups. Group sync keeps working for other groups. Signing in only with Microsoft. When an action needs a fresh sign-in, people who sign in only with Microsoft can sign in with Microsoft again for it. Corrected texts. The notes about who can see and reveal what were corrected throughout. Platform security hardened. Improved key handling, notices when a Passkey is added from a recovery code, and the last Passkey of a platform administrator can no longer be removed.
