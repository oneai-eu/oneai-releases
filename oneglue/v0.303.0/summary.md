OneGlue v0.303.0: External people with organization roles now see and reveal what their role allows, and anyone who loses a Passkey has a way back. Important: Organization viewers, organization contributors and tenant viewers can now reveal all passwords with default access in their organization(s). For entries that only certain people should see, please set them to "restricted" ("Reveal default → Restricted"). External people with organization roles. They now see and reveal what their role allows. Imported entries behave like entries created in OneGlue. A way back after a lost Passkey – for everyone. You receive an e-mailed link to register a new Passkey. Recovery codes alone no longer create a Passkey. Invitations always match the current access. If an admin changes the role or blocks the account before the invitation is redeemed, the invitation is void. A clear warning before far-reaching roles. Before a tenant-wide role is given, you see a warning with numbers and confirm once more – especially for addresses outside your own domains. Reactivating and re-inviting works again. This includes accounts and memberships with an end date. Expired accounts see a clear notice. Access ends when access ends. Open sessions and remote sessions are ended within about a minute. Tenant roles are assigned in OneGlue, not through Microsoft Entra groups. Group sync keeps working for other groups. Signing in only with Microsoft. Users who sign in only with Microsoft can confirm sensitive actions by signing in with Microsoft again. Corrected texts. The notes about who can see and reveal what were corrected throughout. Platform security hardened. Improved key handling, notices when a Passkey is added from a recovery code, and the last Passkey of a platform administrator can no longer be removed.
