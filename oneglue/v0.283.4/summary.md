OneGlue v0.283.4: Your customers can now finish onboarding with their own passkey sign-in. Their own access, right at the end of onboarding. If you offered access when inviting, the customer sets it up at the end: confirmation by fingerprint, PIN or phone, plus emergency codes shown once. From then on they sign in with their passkey and see only their own organization. Login details at the end — optional. In the last step the customer can enter login details. They are stored encrypted. Continue later. Customers who already submitted can return via the invitation link and set up their access afterwards. Certificates: certificate files without a private key stay directly downloadable. Files that contain a key are sealed automatically and shown only after a logged reveal. Invitation and code emails are protected against repeated requests.
