OneGlue v0.283.2 lets you invite new customers to get started: from an organization's page, or right when you create it, the customer receives an e-mail with a link to about ten minutes of simple, guided questions in their own language — address, further sites, IT contact, billing address, devices, documents, and the services and providers they use — and their answers land in their organization as locations, contacts, devices and documents, marked as customer-provided. At the very end they can optionally enter login details, which are stored encrypted and which they cannot view again. You see whether a customer is invited, filling it in or has submitted, can send a new invitation or end the onboarding, and can offer a permanent passkey sign-in for their own organization; a new invitation permission lets colleagues without an administrator role send invitations. Locked passwords are better protected: only tenant administrators and people explicitly entrusted with an entry can change its permissions or unlock it, locking removes general role grants, and locked passwords can no longer be used through shared credentials. Every uploaded file is now stored, checked and only then released, and private keys in PEM format uploaded with SSL certificates are stored encrypted. Fixed: a short interruption after uploading files, pasting images into documents, saving an organization's single sign-on settings, and clearer reporting of failed sign-ins.
