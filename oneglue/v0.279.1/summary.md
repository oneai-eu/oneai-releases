OneGlue v0.279.0 makes the reach of a role visible where it is granted: every tenant-wide role now states that it applies to all organizations — on the invite screen, in the role-change dialog and in the superadmin area, with the organization count shown when inviting — and granting one for the first time asks for a short confirmation that summarises who receives it and what it covers. People pickers, permission overviews, sharing lists, naming prefixes and group details now follow the organization you are working in, so lists are shorter and the choice is unambiguous, while your own access stays visible and Tenant-Admins keep the full picture. From this release on, every version must pass a dedicated organization-boundary check before it ships, to the test environment or to production.
