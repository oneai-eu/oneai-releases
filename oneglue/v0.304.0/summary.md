OneGlue v0.304.0: Microsoft sign-in is safer, Microsoft Entra only syncs users and groups, and restricted ITGlue entries stay restricted on import. Safer Microsoft sign-in. Actions that need a fresh sign-in now ask people who sign in with Microsoft to sign in with Microsoft again (within the last 5 minutes). Sessions started with Microsoft end after 24 hours at the latest. Microsoft Entra syncs users and groups only. Entra never assigns roles – neither tenant-wide nor in an organization. It only fills custom groups that carry no tenant-wide permissions. Roles are assigned in OneGlue. Bulk invitation from an Entra group. You choose the role in the dialog yourself; "no role" is possible. OneGlue no longer guesses a role from the group. Automatic Microsoft linking only for an allowed domain. At Microsoft sign-in, an existing account is linked automatically by its e-mail address only if the address is verified and belongs to an allowed domain. Accounts that are already linked, linking from your own profile, and invitation links are not affected. Restricted ITGlue entries stay restricted. When importing, entries marked "restricted" in ITGlue stay restricted in OneGlue, including the passwords and keys that belong to them (SSL keys, Wi-Fi keys). If an export has no "restricted" column, the import stops instead of making everything visible. Clearer invitations and notices. Expired invitations show a clear message. Tenant admins are told when an external person sends an invitation. Organization admins from outside your domains are judged by your tenant's domains. Platform security hardened. Platform administrators register a new Passkey after a recovery only through an e-mailed link, and switching tenants keeps the type of the current sign-in. Corrected texts. Notes and error messages in the invitation, recovery and sign-in flows now say what OneGlue actually does.
