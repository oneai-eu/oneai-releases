OneGlue v0.221.0 is a usability release. Device (configuration) detail pages now follow a fixed, consistent layout — the device's identity, connection actions, and credentials always come first, documentation next, and automation/advanced tools last, with sections that don't apply simply omitted. Shared-credential labels are unified across the app, and each credential section on a device gains a one-line explanation of what it is. Favorites now sort to the top on contacts, locations, and flexible assets (matching configurations, passwords, and networks), while lists with a meaningful order (documents by last edited, domains and certificates by expiry) keep their order and just show the star. All changes are presentation-only — no change to permissions or to who can see or do what.
