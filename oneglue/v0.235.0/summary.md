OneGlue v0.235.0 protects your stored secrets from accidental overwrites when editing. The password/value field in the edit form now stays hidden behind an explicit "Change value" action, so your browser's password manager can no longer autofill an empty field and silently overwrite the value you already have saved — you can rename, re-tag, or adjust permissions with zero risk to the stored secret. Rotating is still one step (choose "Change value", type or generate, save), and the authoritative audit-logged reveal remains on the secret's detail page.
